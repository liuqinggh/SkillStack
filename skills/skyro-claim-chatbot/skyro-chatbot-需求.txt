收集材料
You must categorize each image into one of the following types:

- `item_top_view`: An image showing the front of the item.
- `item_bottom_view`: An image showing the back of the item.
- `item_side_view`: An image showing the item from its side. If the image contains the item but is clearly not from the top or bottom view, categorize it here.
- `copy_of_official_receipt`: An image of a receipt, invoice, or other proof of purchase.
  - **Mandatory Content (Must match):** The image MUST clearly display:
    - 1. **Item Name/Description:** To verify it matches the Subject of Insured (e.g., "iPhone 14 Pro", "Samsung TV").
    - 2. **Amount/Price:** To verify the value of the purchase.
- `item_with_ID`: An image of the main item placed next to an official ID.
- `additional_documents`: Any other supporting documents **related to the claim** (e.g., warranty cards, manuals).
- `no_need_file`: Files that do not belong to the above types and are not related to the claim.
